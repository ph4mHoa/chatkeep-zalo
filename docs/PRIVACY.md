# ChatKeep for Zalo privacy policy

*Last updated: 25 September 2026*

ChatKeep for Zalo is a Chrome extension that saves the Zalo chats you choose, from Zalo Web
(chat.zalo.me), as files on your computer. This policy explains what it does with your data.

## What ChatKeep reads

When you scan your chat list or start an export, ChatKeep reads, from the chat.zalo.me tab where
you are logged in:

- the list of your chats (names, whether a chat is a group, member counts, dates of activity);
- for the chats you selected: the messages with their senders and times, replies, mentions,
  captions, link previews, and the addresses of photos, videos, voice messages and files;
- the display names of the people in those chats, which Zalo Web looks up for it the way it does
  for a group's member list. Only display names are kept; no phone numbers or other profile
  details.

It reads only what Zalo Web has in your browser (or loads for it from Zalo when asked for older
history), and only while a scan or an export that you started is running. It does not open chats,
mark messages as seen, send messages, or change anything in your account.

## Where your data goes

Nowhere outside your computer.

- Messages are kept in the extension's local storage in your browser (IndexedDB), so later exports
  only need to read what is new.
- Exported files, and the photos and files you chose to include, are saved to your Downloads
  folder under `Zalo_Export/`. Media files are downloaded directly from Zalo's servers.
- ChatKeep has no server. It sends no messages, files, analytics or usage data to the developer or
  to anyone else, and it loads no code from the internet.

## Sharing and sale

ChatKeep does not sell, share or transfer your data, and does not use it for advertising, credit
decisions or any purpose other than the export you asked for.

## Deleting your data

- Exported files: delete them from your Downloads folder.
- The archive inside the browser: remove the extension (chrome://extensions → ChatKeep for Zalo →
  Remove). Chrome deletes its stored data with it.

## Permissions

ChatKeep asks only for what the export needs: access to chat.zalo.me to read the chats you select
there, downloads to save the files, storage for the archive and the export's progress, and the side
panel for its interface.
